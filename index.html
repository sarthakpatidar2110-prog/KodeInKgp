IITKGP FEST

PREMIER CULTURAL FEST | JAN 2027

ABOUT FEST

Get ready for IIT Kharagpur’s premier tech extravaganza, hosted by KodeinKGP! This fest is the ultimate collision of innovation and entertainment, featuring grueling 24-hour software hackathons alongside adrenaline-pumping hardware showdowns like high-speed RC car racing and combat robotics. Whether you are building next-gen apps or engineering race-ready rovers, the competition is fierce. But it’s not just about the grind—after days of relentless building, the event transforms into a massive celebration. The fest culminates in an epic Pro-Nite, where a headline musical artist will take the stage for an electrifying live performance to bring the house down and close out the fest in style!

EVENTS

🟣TechTriad Hackathon: A 36-hour coding marathon organized by KodeinKGP where developers collaborate to build impactful, real-world tech solutions.
🟣Robowars: A flagship engineering competition where heavy, custom-built remote-controlled bots battle in an arena.
🟣Guest Lectures: Interactive sessions and talks led by renowned scientists, tech leaders, and industry pioneers to inspire students.
🟣Tech Exhibitions: Interactive displays of cutting-edge technology, including humanoid robots and AR/VR systems, showcased by companies and research centers.

TYPES OF TICKETS

Silver: The Explorer Pass

Designed for the casual attendee who wants to soak in the atmosphere of the hackathons and catch the concert without breaking the bank. Tech Access: General admission to the robotics competition stands and hackathon spectator zones. Concert Access: Standard standing room or lawn access for the evening musical performance. Food Perks: Complimentary water stations, basic energy bars, and standard event snacks.


Gold: The Innovator Pass

The sweet spot for attendees who want a comfortable, elevated experience during the long hours of the tech events and a great view of the stage at night. Tech Access: Guaranteed priority seating at the main robotics arena and access to exclusive developer workshops. Concert Access: Early entry to the venue and access to a reserved, dedicated seating area. Food Perks: Unlimited coffee, tea, and an assortment of premium cookies, gourmet biscuits, and pastries to keep the energy up during the hackathon.


Platinum: The Visionary VIP Pass

The ultimate all-access pass offering white-glove treatment across both the daytime tech events and the nighttime entertainment. Tech Access: Exclusive access to the VIP tech lounge, private networking sessions with hackathon judges, and pit-lane access at the robotics competition. Concert Access: Front-row pit access, a dedicated VIP viewing balcony, and an exclusive backstage tour. Food Perks: An open bar featuring free premium drinks (craft mocktails, cocktails, and specialty energy drinks) alongside a fully catered gourmet meal.
